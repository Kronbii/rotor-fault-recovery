In 110 PX4 SITL flights (24 of them for calibration), the diagnoser detected 40/40 partial losses of 19-48 \% (median delay 120 ms, 95th percentile 128 ms), isolated 40/40 to the right motor and estimated the loss with a median error of 0.5 points; it detected 16/16 complete losses after a median 132 ms, all before the vehicle tilted past 90 deg (median 486 ms). No false alarm occurred in 22 min of fault-free flight. PX4's failure detector raised no flag for the partial losses, and for the complete losses its attitude-limit check fired 16/16 times after a median 664 ms, in 16 of them after the tilt had passed 90 deg. Without the adaptive residual scale the median delays drop to 40 ms (partial) and 20 ms (complete), but 4 of 56 faults are then isolated to the wrong motor. After a partial loss PX4 held altitude in 15/16 runs below 35 \% loss and in 6/24 runs above. PX4's own failure motor off injection changed nothing in 2/2 flights with the default CA\_FAILURE\_MODE = 0; with CA\_FAILURE\_MODE = 1 it stopped the motor and the vehicle flipped in 4/4 (attitude check after a median 660 ms).
